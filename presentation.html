Context & Objectives
Problem Statement & Real-World Motivation
Slide 2 / 12
1. The Industry Challenge
  • • Thousands of unstructured customer reviews arrive daily across e-commerce & retail.
  • • Manual reading is physically impossible and subjective.
  • • Simple binary scoring (Pos/Neg) hides operational issues like broken deliveries or slow customer care.
2. Limitations of Existing Tools
  • • Commercial APIs (OpenAI, AWS Comprehend) require costly subscriptions, have rate limits, and leak private data.
  • • Naive keyword searches fail on negations ("not bad", "never recommend") and sarcasm.
  • • Academic Viva needs full mathematical explainability, not black-box APIs.
3. Our Solution
  • • 100% offline-ready NLP intelligence pipeline with zero API billing.
  • • Dual intelligence: Lexicon (VADER) + Machine Learning (Scikit-Learn TF-IDF).
  • • Opinion Mining via TextBlob subjectivity scoring (0.0 Fact vs 1.0 Opinion).
  • • Automated Aspect Extraction with urgency triage priority.
Pipeline Overview
System Architecture: 6-Stage Intelligence Flow
Slide 3 / 12
Stage 1 to 3: Ingestion & Lexicons
  • 1. Multi-Modal Ingest: Manual text entry, microphone voice dictation (HTML5 Web Speech API), or bulk CSV uploads.
  • 2. Morphological Preprocessing: Tokenization, regex noise cleaning, smart stopword elimination, and dictionary lemmatization.
  • 3. Rule-Based Lexicon (VADER): Valence intensity analysis with dynamic compound threshold tuning.
Stage 4 to 6: ML & Actionable Insights
  • 4. Opinion Mining (TextBlob): Subjectivity quantification separating technical specs from emotional claims.
  • 5. ML Classification: TF-IDF N-grams with Multinomial Naive Bayes and Logistic Regression.
  • 6. Aspect-Based ABSA: Domain tagging (Quality, Shipping, Support, Pricing) + automated priority action plans.
NLP Fundamentals
Text Preprocessing: Lemmatization vs. Stemming
Slide 4 / 12
Lemmatization vs. Stemming (Key Viva Defense)
  • • Stemming (e.g. Porter Stemmer): Crudely truncates word suffixes using static heuristic rules. Produces invalid non-dictionary words:
    "caring" → "car"  |  "troubled" → "troubl"
  • • Morphological Lemmatization (Our Engine): Leverages dictionary vocabulary and morphological rules to resolve inflections to authentic root lemmas:
    "caring" → "care"  |  "better" → "good"  |  "worst" → "bad"
  • • Ensures clean semantic matching for aspect noun extraction.
Smart Stopword Removal & Negation Handling
  • • Strips non-informative words ("the", "is", "at", "which").
  • • Critical Negation Retention: Preserves sentiment polarity inverters: "not", "never", "barely", "hardly".
    Example: "not good" is preserved to avoid false positive classification!
  • • Regex tokenizer captures alphanumeric tokens while dropping punctuation noise.
Rule-Based Intelligence
VADER Sentiment Analysis & Compound Formula
Slide 5 / 12
The VADER Compound Metric Formula

Normalized weighted composite score bounded between -1.0 (extremely negative) and +1.0 (extremely positive):

Compound Score (c) = x / √(x² + α)
  • • x: Sum of valence scores of constituent lexicon tokens.
  • • α: Normalization parameter (experimentally calibrated to α ≈ 15).
  • • Dynamic Slider: Boundary threshold τ defaults to ±0.05, adjustable from 0.00 to 0.50 live in the UI.
Micro-Sentiment Heuristics
  • • Punctuation Booster: "Great!!!" scores higher than "Great".
  • • Capitalization Intensifier: "AMAZING" produces higher positive valence than "amazing".
  • • Degree Adverbs: Handles boosting ("extremely fast") and dampening ("marginally fast").
  • • Ratios: Computes exact Pos/Neg/Neu breakdown ratios.
Opinion Mining
TextBlob Opinion Mining: Subjectivity vs. Fact
Slide 6 / 12
Subjectivity Spectrum [0.0 to 1.0]
  • • Score = 0.0 (Pure Objective / Fact):
    Statements containing factual specifications or logistics.
    Example: "The laptop arrived in 2 business days with 16GB RAM."
    Sentiment is neutral, subjectivity = 0.0.
  • • Score = 1.0 (Pure Subjective / Opinion):
    Statements expressing personal feelings or aesthetics.
    Example: "The keyboard design is gorgeous and feels like pure heaven."
    Sentiment is high, subjectivity = 0.95.
Commercial Business Value
  • • High Negative + Low Subjectivity = Hardware / Delivery Defect:
    Immediate operational bug to be fixed by the engineering team!
  • • High Negative + High Subjectivity = Customer Taste / Expectations:
    Handled by PR / marketing rather than product recalls.
  • • Allows automated routing of tickets to the exact corporate department.
Machine Learning Engine
Scikit-Learn TF-IDF N-Grams & Classifiers
Slide 7 / 12
Feature Vectorizer: TF-IDF (1, 2) N-Grams
  • • Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency: Down-weights common words while boosting distinctive sentiment terms.
  • • Unigrams + Bigrams: Captures single words ("fast") and combined pairs ("not fast", "very slow").
  • • L2 vector normalization scales input feature vectors smoothly for classification.
Dual Model Benchmark Architecture
  • • Multinomial Logistic Regression: Linear decision boundary with softmax probability estimates and L2 regularization.
  • • Multinomial Naive Bayes (MNB): Fast probabilistic classifier applying Bayes' rule with Laplace smoothing.
  • • Consensus Engine: Automatically detects when VADER rule-based scoring disagrees with ML statistical models.

Examiner Viva Prep
Viva Voce Defense: Key Technical Q&A
Slide 11 / 12
Top Examiner Questions & Crisp Answers
  • Q: Why combine Rule-Based VADER with ML?
    Ans: VADER handles zero-shot cold start and micro-sentiment without labeled data. ML models learn domain-specific vocabulary patterns. Comparing both provides multi-model consensus and flags discrepancies.
  • Q: How is VADER compound score normalized?
    Ans: Normalized via formula c = x / √(x² + α) where α ≈ 15 binds it smoothly between -1.0 and +1.0.
Architectural Defense Points
  • Q: How does SQLite work in a Serverless environment?
    Ans: SQLite stores in /tmp on Vercel with automatic table initialization on cold-start. For local environments, it persists directly to reviews.db with 1-click CSV export.
  • Q: How does TextBlob subjectivity differ from polarity?
    Ans: Polarity measures emotional valence (-1 to +1). Subjectivity measures factual grounding (0.0 = verifiable facts, 1.0 = subjective emotional opinions).
